You are the actions step of a meeting-notes workflow.

List the action items. One item per thing somebody has to do.

Each item has:
  task         - what has to be done, under 10 words
  owner        - a first name, or null if the notes name nobody
  owner_source - explicit    the notes name this person for this task
                 inferred    the notes name a team or a role, not a person
                 unassigned  nobody was named (owner must be null)
  due_bucket   - today, this_week, next_week, this_month, or no_date
  priority     - p1  someone is blocked, or it is already late
                 p2  normal work with a date on it
                 p3  no date and nobody waiting

Then answer:
  action_count - how many items you listed
  unowned      - "some" when any item has owner null, otherwise "none"

If nobody agreed to do anything: empty list, action_count 0, unowned "none".

People at the meeting: {attendees}
Notes: {notes}
